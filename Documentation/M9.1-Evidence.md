## Forensic Audit Summary
- **Root Cause Identified:** The original document picker failure occurred because `CFBundleDocumentTypes` lacked the required `CFBundleTypeRole` key (`Viewer`), causing LaunchServices to treat the document handler registration as incomplete or invalid, and `LSSupportsOpeningDocumentsInPlace` was missing. Furthermore, exporting both `gguf` and `GGUF` extensions under both `UTExportedTypeDeclarations` and `UTImportedTypeDeclarations` with `LSHandlerRank = Owner` ensures LaunchServices binds `.gguf` files directly to `org.ggml.gguf`.
